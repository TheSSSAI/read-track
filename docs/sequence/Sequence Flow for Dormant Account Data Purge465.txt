# 1 Overview

## 1.1 Diagram Id

SEQ-CF-004

## 1.2 Name

Dormant Account Data Purge

## 1.3 Description

A scheduled monthly background job identifies user accounts inactive for over 2 years. It then triggers the existing permanent account deletion service for each identified account to comply with data minimization principles under GDPR.

## 1.4 Type

🔹 ComplianceFlow

## 1.5 Purpose

To enforce the data retention policy by automatically purging stale data, reducing data breach liability and complying with privacy regulations, as per REQ-DAT-001.

## 1.6 Complexity

Medium

## 1.7 Priority

🟡 Medium

## 1.8 Frequency

Monthly

## 1.9 Participants

- REPO-BE-HOST

## 1.10 Key Interactions

- A scheduled Hangfire job runs monthly.
- The job queries the User table for accounts where `lastLoginDate` is older than 2 years.
- For each dormant account, the job enqueues a call to the account deletion service.
- The account deletion service performs the hard delete of all related PII and content, reusing the same logic as the user-initiated flow.
- The action is logged in the system's operational logs for tracking.

## 1.11 Triggers

- A monthly scheduled job is executed.

## 1.12 Outcomes

- Data for inactive users is permanently deleted from the system.
- The system adheres to its data retention policy.

## 1.13 Business Rules

- An account is considered dormant after 2 years of inactivity.
- The purge must be a permanent, hard delete.

## 1.14 Error Scenarios

- The scheduled job fails to run.
- An error occurs during the deletion of a specific user's data, which should be retried.

## 1.15 Integration Points

*No items available*

# 2.0 Details

## 2.1 Diagram Id

SEQ-CF-004-IMPL

## 2.2 Name

Implementation: Automated Dormant Account Data Purge for GDPR Compliance

## 2.3 Description

A detailed technical sequence for a scheduled monthly background job that enforces the data retention policy (REQ-DAT-001) by purging user accounts inactive for over 2 years. The job identifies dormant accounts, enqueues individual deletion tasks for resilience, performs a hard delete of all PII and user-generated content, and records each deletion in the immutable audit trail as per security and compliance requirements.

#### 2.6.1.1 Content

The separation of the main purge job (enqueuer) from the individual deletion jobs is a critical resilience pattern. It prevents a single user's deletion failure from halting the entire batch.
